I am a Postdoctoral researcher in the [Bayesian Deep Learning Group](https://bdl.kaust.edu.sa/), led by [Maurizio Filippone](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=ILUeAloAAAAJ&view_op=list_works&sortby=pubdate) at [KAUST](https://www.kaust.edu.sa/en/). I hold a PhD in Statistics and was advised by [Haavard Rue](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=VJOn_ZkAAAAJ&hl=en&oi=sra) and [David Bolin](https://scholar.google.se/citations?user=GNOopfAAAAAJ&hl=en). My research centers on **Probabilistic Machine Learning**, with a particular focus on **Bayesian computation and workflow**, **latent Gaussian models** and **Uncertainty Quantification for Deep Learning models.** I am interested in developing principled and computationally efficient methods for probabilistic modeling, inference, prediction, and model assessment.
